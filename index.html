About Rahul Industries

Precision Toolroom & Custom Manufacturing

At Rahul Industries, we specialize in high-precision machining job works and custom mould tooling. Based in Makarpura GIDC, Vadodara, we deliver reliable B2B manufacturing backed by modern CNC, VMC, and Wire Cut EDM machinery.

Equipped for both one-off precision prototypes and high-volume job works, our toolroom adheres to strict micron tolerances across pre-hardened tool steels, standard metals, and engineering plastics.
